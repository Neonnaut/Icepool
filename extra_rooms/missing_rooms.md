# Ohm Cave

This room could be accessed through a hole in the ground via Cloud Plaza during a quest to get an "Ohm stone" for Zorof. No screenshots, videos or other captures of this room can be found, but we know it looked like The Beneath.

# Maze Train Station

This place is where the train came to take people to The Beneath Station. It was removed in the 2009 version. We only have a blurry Youtube video of this room.

# The Node

The Node was a big round room with statues in the middle. There were many doors, however only two were accessable, going to the Maze Train Station and The Cave of Shrines. It was removed in the 2009 version. We only have a blurry Youtube video of this room.

# The Cave of Shrines

This was a place filled with some statues. There was a bridge and another entry but they were not accessible. The other entries led to The Node, and the Air Tram Station. This room was removed in the 2009 version. Some of the statues and the bridge were reused in Everflow Cavern. We only have a blurry Youtube video of this room.
