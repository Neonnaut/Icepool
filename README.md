# Icepool

Hello friends and welcome to “Icepool”. “Icepool” is a collection of recovered and recreated Spineworld assets and artwork.

Spineworld was an MMO game aimed at older teens created by Swedish company Playdo. That's Playdo not Play-doh, by the way.

## We need YOU!

We do not have full backups of 2009+ Spineworld.

Progress is progressing, but with your help we could have it released even sooner.

If you have your old laptop/computer from when you played this game when you were young, you may have the key to unlock all of Spineworld.

A reward of up to $100 will be given for any new Spineworld files.

If you think you may be able to help, please send a message to the server-owner "Cash" on the discord server found at spineworld.uk/s/support.
